# ☀️ SKYBET SOLAR INDUSTRIES
### Smart Solar Solutions | Premium Solar Products | Clean Energy

**Skybet Solar Industries** is a modern, mobile-first solar product shopping platform designed to make solar equipment accessible through a seamless digital shopping experience.

The platform allows customers to explore solar products, browse product categories, search for equipment, discover featured products, manage wishlists, and add products to their shopping cart.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://pwa.skybetsolarindustries.com/shop

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🛍️ Product Shopping
- Browse a wide range of solar products.
- Explore featured and recommended products.
- View product images, names, and prices.
- Add products to the shopping cart.
- Manage favorite products through the wishlist.

### 🔍 Product Search & Discovery
- Search products by name and model.
- Browse products through dedicated categories.
- Filter products based on product type.
- Sort products by price (Low to High / High to Low).
- Easily discover available products.

### 📦 Product Categories
- Solar Panels
- LED Lights
- Batteries
- Inverters
- Solar Accessories
- Other Solar Solutions

### 📱 Mobile-First Experience
- Responsive mobile-friendly interface.
- Progressive Web App (PWA) experience.
- Easy navigation and product discovery.
- Optimized product browsing.
- User-friendly shopping interface.

### 🎨 Modern UI/UX
- Clean and professional design.
- Blue and white visual theme.
- Modern product cards.
- Smooth navigation and interactive elements.
- Consistent shopping experience.

## 📂 Project Overview

The platform is designed to support the complete online solar product shopping workflow, including:

- Product discovery and browsing.
- Category-based product organization.
- Product search and filtering.
- Wishlist management.
- Shopping cart management.
- Backend API integration.
- Product and catalog management.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of Skybet Solar Industries is to simplify the online discovery and shopping of solar energy products.

The platform provides customers with an accessible digital marketplace where they can explore solar equipment, compare product prices, discover suitable solutions, and shop conveniently from their mobile devices.
